Dear Editor,

My coauthors Yilong Hou, Zhengpu Zhao, Yi Li and I are submitting our manuscript, "HAL-MLE Log-Splines Density Estimation (Part I: Univariate)," for consideration for publication in _The Annals of Statistics_.

This paper studies nonparametric density estimation through the Highly Adaptive Lasso framework, establishing asymptotic linearity, pointwise asymptotic normality, and a uniform convergence rate for univariate HAL-MLE while clarifying its connection to classical total-variation-penalized methods.

Thank you for your consideration.
